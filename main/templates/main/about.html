About Golden Crust

From a home kitchen to your table

Golden Crust started in 2021 in a small kitchen in Bacnotan. What began as pandesal for neighbors became a daily stop for students, families, and offices around DMMMSU.

We still bake the same way — by hand, in small batches, with ingredients we trust. No shortcuts. Just good bread and honest pastries.

Every morning the ovens light up before the sun. That’s how we keep the quality consistent and the smell of fresh bread in the air.
